# ClimateCapital AI P0 Evidence and Methodology Lock

> **Status:** Locked and reviewed on 2026-09-01
> **Authority:** This document is the authoritative P0 analytical methodology.
> It records reviewed evidence findings separately from the evidence-driven
> Product and Design changes in `docs/product/`.

## Locked Outcome

P0 is an evidence-first funding decision-support experience. It does not produce
a synthetic Funding Priority score, project rank, or optimized recommendation.
The authoritative deterministic behavior is limited to governed project facts,
evidence-state classification, analyst-controlled full-request membership,
Available Budget validation, exact budget arithmetic, and supported scenario
comparison.

The absence of a comparable numeric risk, benefit, or vulnerability measure is a
methodological result, not a gap to hide with imputation, confidence points, or AI.

## Evidence-State Contract

Every analytical field must have one of these roles:

| Role | Meaning | P0 treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FACT** | Source-faithful governed value, or a reproducible ClimateCapital derivation identified as derived | May participate in deterministic identity, family derivation, validation of analyst-provided membership, and budget arithmetic when its use is documented |
| **CONTEXTUAL EVIDENCE** | Relevant evidence that helps an analyst understand place, purpose, hazard, or vulnerability but does not establish benefit, need, eligibility, or priority | Display with source, vintage, coverage, limitation, and missingness; do not score |
| **RESEARCH-ONLY EVIDENCE** | Evidence whose historical fit, geometry semantics, source quality, or comparability is insufficient for governed P0 analysis | May support methodology/provenance explanation; do not use in membership or arithmetic |
| **UNAVAILABLE / UNSUPPORTED** | A value does not exist, cannot be reproduced, is missing for the project, or is not supported for the claimed use | Show explicitly; never fabricate, impute, coerce to zero, or penalize |

Evidence confidence describes the strength of a classification, association, or
source linkage. It never represents need, severity, benefit, priority, or
decision correctness. Problem Score A/B/C/D association strength is provenance
strength only.

## Deterministic P0 Funding-Plan Method

### Scenario inputs

- Available Budget, denominated in whole dollars.
- Analyst-controlled inclusion or removal of records from the explicitly active
  12-record P0 analytical family. Projects outside that family remain in the
  governed-universe audit but cannot enter a P0 Funding Plan unless a later
  governed methodology decision changes the active-family contract.
- No Importance weights, Climate Risk weights, Community Vulnerability weights,
  score transformations, or optimization objective.

Gemini may translate an explicit analyst command such as a budget change or named
project inclusion/removal into a pending structured action. It may not originate
which records should be funded, generate facts, create an objective, or apply a
change without confirmation.

### Validation and arithmetic

For each confirmed scenario the deterministic system must:

1. Validate that every included ID belongs to the active 12-record P0 analytical
   family; reject unknown IDs and governed IDs outside that family.
2. Reject duplicate IDs and any mutation of governed request amounts.
3. Include or remove the entire governed request; partial funding is unsupported.
4. Sum included governed requests exactly in integer dollars.
5. Require `included_total <= Available Budget` before confirmation.
6. Return included IDs, not-included IDs, included count, included total, Available
   Budget, and remaining amount.
7. Preserve the prior confirmed scenario when attempted inputs are invalid or over
   budget.

The deterministic system validates but never chooses membership, and no
combination is automatically preferred. Alphabetical, source-order, request,
purpose, evidence-role, and missingness controls are presentation aids only.

### Scenario lifecycle and comparison

- The $125,000,000 Watershed Projects envelope is the default Historical Envelope
  context. It does not define the family or force any record into a plan.
- Before the analyst confirms membership, the workspace is an unconfirmed working
  plan and is not a ClimateCapital recommendation.
- The first confirmed full-request plan under the $125,000,000 context becomes the
  immutable current-session **Session Reference Plan**. It is analyst-created and
  is not a historical baseline, Historical City Recommendation, or City plan.
- **Current Confirmed Plan** means whichever analyst-confirmed plan is currently
  active: the Session Reference Plan or the confirmed What-If Scenario.
- P0 may retain at most one confirmed analyst-created What-If Scenario. A What-If
  may change Available Budget and/or project membership.
- Supported comparison is limited to budget, included total, remainder, count,
  membership changes, governed request-dollar changes, and transparent evidence
  availability or context. It has no score, rank, optimized objective value, or
  inferred benefit delta.
- Either reviewed confirmed plan may receive the current-session Reviewed
  Draft designation with the existing non-official and non-persistent disclaimer.
- The January 2026 Historical City Recommendation remains a separately sourced and
  stored descriptive benchmark. It never supplies or changes project evidence,
  analytical-family definition, analyst membership, validation, or scenario
  arithmetic.

## Missing and Unsupported Evidence

- Missing is never zero and never evidence of low need.
- Missing geometry or evidence does not remove a record from the active 12-record
  analytical family or Funding Plan controls and never implies ineligibility.
- No risk, vulnerability, benefit, or confidence value is imputed.
- No record receives a penalty or advantage because another record has richer
  evidence.
- Evidence confidence and Problem Score association strength never enter budget
  arithmetic or membership logic.
- The UI must distinguish field unavailable, unsupported metric, not applicable,
  contextual evidence, and research-only evidence.
- Gemini must use the same explicit states and must not fill gaps with plausible
  language or inferred locations.

## Outputs Explicitly Removed from P0

- Numeric Funding Priority score.
- Project priority rank.
- Quantitative Climate Risk score.
- Quantitative Community Vulnerability or Community Equity score.
- Expected flood-reduction benefit.
- People or structures benefited unless future governed evidence explicitly
  establishes a metric in a later methodology revision.
- Importance weights or any weight editor.
- Optimizer objective, optimized membership, tie-breaking, and optimization
  infeasibility claims.
- “Recommended Projects” terminology for ClimateCapital-selected records.

Use **Projects**, **Included in Plan**, **Available Projects**, **Not Included in
Plan**, and **analyst-reviewed scenario** instead. “Historical City
Recommendation” remains reserved for the isolated published benchmark.

## Product Reconciliation Required by This Lock

The following earlier assumptions are superseded because the evidence gate did
not support their required inputs:

- rule-derived analytical eligibility as a scored/optimized cohort;
- expected flood-reduction benefit as a common P0 signal;
- numeric Funding Priority and rank;
- approved Importance weights and weight-only scenarios;
- optimizer-authoritative membership and the prohibition on manual project
  inclusion/removal;
- Recommended/Not Included grouping as an optimizer result; and
- Gemini-originated weight proposals or explanations of nonexistent ranks and
  objectives.

The following remain intact:

- Austin Watershed as the P0 pilot and all 37 records as the governed universe;
- January 21, 2026 as the historical decision context;
- the $125,000,000 Projects envelope as historical/default budget context;
- full-request rather than partial-funding treatment;
- structural isolation of the Historical City Recommendation from project
  evidence, analytical-family definition, analyst membership, validation, and
  scenario arithmetic;
- Map → Projects → Funding Plan, non-map access, explicit missingness, source and
  vintage disclosure, one Session Reference Plan plus at most one What-If,
  Reviewed Draft, and accessible recovery behavior; and
- deterministic authority for governed facts, validation, arithmetic, and state,
  with Gemini limited to grounded explanation and reversible interaction.

## Change Control

Later evidence may add a metric only through an explicit methodology revision that
documents its source, historical fit, coverage, comparability, missingness,
transformation, and effect. Application code, architecture, pipelines, weights,
scores, and optimization are outside this lock.
